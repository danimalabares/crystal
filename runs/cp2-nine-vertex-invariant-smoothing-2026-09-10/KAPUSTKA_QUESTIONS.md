1. **The no-degeneration claim itself.** Please state precisely which object is claimed not to degenerate to `X = Proj A`
   (the Stanley–Reisner fourfold of ℂP²₉ in ℙ⁸): (a) a Kum₂-type fourfold with a square-2 polarisation, embedded by the
   complete linear system; (b) any smooth fourfold in the Hilbert-scheme component(s) through `[X]`; (c) only fibres
   carrying the `H`-action (`|H| = 54`) or the `S₃`-action. The three statements need different proofs.

2. **The dimension count.** Which two dimensions are being compared? Our exact numbers: `T_{[X]}Hilb(ℙ⁸) = H⁰(N_X)`
   has dimension 93 (not an upper bound for a component; `T²` has dimension 126); the `H`-fixed locus of the Hilbert
   scheme is smooth of dimension 5 at `[X]` (3 torus + 2); the `S₃`-fixed locus has tangent dimension 23 but may be
   obstructed (`(T²)^{S₃} = 14`). The polarised moduli of Kum₂-type fourfolds with `q(H) = 2` is 4-dimensional, and its
   Hilbert-scheme image has dimension 4 + 80 = 84 if generic automorphism groups are finite. Which inequality is claimed,
   and does it use the tangent space 93 (fine) or the invariant tangent space 2 (must be compared with the *equivariant*
   moduli, not with embedded families)?

3. **Lattice input for an `H`-equivariant obstruction.** For `H = F₃² ⋊ S₃` acting on a Kum₂-type fourfold `Y` with an

   we need this number to compare with 2); (iii) whether the translation subgroup `F₃²` could act trivially on `H²`
   (the kernel of `Aut(Y) → O(H²)` is `(ℤ/3)⁴ ⋊ ℤ/2` for Kum₂, so this is possible); (iv) the reference for the Torelli
   statement used (Markman; note Hodge-theoretic Torelli fails for Kum_n, monodromy data are needed).

4. **The fixed-point structure.** Our special fibre has, for each order-3 translation, nine fixed points splitting into
   reduced points in the invariant family. Do the holomorphic/topological Lefschetz numbers of an order-3 symplectic or
